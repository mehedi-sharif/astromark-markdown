items:
  - question: Are my documents really just markdown files?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Every Quill document is a plain `.md` file on disk. You can open
      it in any text editor, put it in Git, or copy it to another machine —
      Quill never wraps your words in a database or a proprietary container.
  - question: Do I need an account to try it?
    answer: >-
      No. Your first draft needs no account and no payment. You can write,
      preview, and export without signing up; sync and history arrive when you
      decide to go Pro.
  - question: Can I bring existing markdown into Quill?
    answer: >-
      Always. Drop a folder of `.md` files into your workspace and Quill
      indexes them as-is — no import step, no conversion, no reformatting of
      your frontmatter.
  - question: What happens to my files if I stop paying?
    answer: >-
      They stay exactly where they are: on your machine, as plain text. Cancel
      anytime and your local documents keep working. Sync and version history
      pause, but your words never do.
  - question: Does Quill work offline?
    answer: >-
      Fully. Because documents are local files, Quill writes offline by
      default and syncs when you're back online. A dead train tunnel is not a
      writing emergency.
  - question: Is my content used to train AI models?
    answer: >-
      No. We do not train on, scan, or share your documents with any model
      provider. Your text is yours and stays private to your account and
      devices.
