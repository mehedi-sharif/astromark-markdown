## Why writers switch to Quill

Most writing tools want to own your words. They bundle them into databases,
hide them behind rich-text editors, and quietly make leaving hard. Quill
does the opposite: **your documents are plain markdown files**, yours to
move, version, and read in any text editor on earth.

That sounds small. It changes everything.

- **No migration tax.** Copy a `.md` file anywhere — it works. Paste it back
  into Quill — it still works.
- **Your archive outlives the app.** Ten years from now, your drafts open in
  whatever comes next. Plain text has no expiry date.
- **Version control is free.** Drop your folder in Git and every edit is a
  commit you can diff, branch, and roll back.

> The best writing tool is the one you'll still be able to read in a decade.
> Quill bets on the format, not the lock-in.
